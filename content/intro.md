Cellular automata are grids of simple cells that all follow the same local rule. This project walks one idea through four generations of the model: elementary automata (one dimension, 256 possible rules), life-like automata (two dimensions, Conway's Game of Life and 131,072 relatives), Lenia (continuous states and smooth kernels), and neural cellular automata, where the rule is a small neural network learned by gradient descent.

Then the arrow is reversed: given observed dynamics, can we recover the rule?
